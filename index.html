EcoCity

Clean the street. Sort the waste. Get rewarded.

Playable prototype of the EcoCycle Hub PRD — pickups, weights and ₦ payouts are simulated.

How to play

  1. Walk the streets. Click or tap the ground to walk there, or use W A S D (or the joystick on touch) — or click your character to have them walk around on their own. Pick up waste by clicking it — your EcoHero walks over and collects it — or use E / the Collect button when close.
  2. Take it to the Recycling Hub and run the sorting mini-game — learn which bin each material belongs in.
  3. Staged smart bags wait at your EcoHome. Press Request pickup in the Rewards panel and a partner truck will come and verify your recyclables.
  4. Earn score, coins, XP, ₦ rewards and badges. Upgrade your bag, shoes and gloves in the shop.
  5. Talk to the street. Mama Ngozi, DJ Ada, Auntie Bisi and Chidi have their own plans, moods and memories of your visit. Walk up and press E — or click them — for a live conversation.
  6. Watch the drain-risk meter. If too much litter clogs the gutters, heavy rain can flood the street — clean up to stop it.
